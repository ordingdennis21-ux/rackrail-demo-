Process

Four steps. No downtime.

  1. 01

    Enquiry and call

    You describe the system and the problem. We clarify rail profile, access and time window, weekends included.

  2. 02

    Survey and assessment

    Laser-based survey to 0.1 mm. We assess wear, corrugation and settled joints.

  3. 03

    Work during operation

    Grinding, welding, refurbishment. Aisle by aisle, while the rest of the system keeps running.

  4. 04

    Report and recommendation

    Before-and-after documentation with measured values and a maintenance interval recommendation.

Services

Grinding is our core.

The running surface decides smoothness, wear and availability. We bring it back into shape.

Core service
01

Grinding & Reconditioning

Corrugation, roll-over and settled joints removed before they cause noise, wear and downtime. Guided grinding machine of our own design, with spark guard and extraction.

  • Corrugation & roll-over
  • Settled joints
  • Own grinding machine
  • Spark guard & extraction
02

Welding & Build-up

Rail joints welded properly, MIG/MAG including CMT, TIG and manual arc, depending on profile and load. Profile restored, joints closed.

03

Measuring & Report

Laser-based survey to 0.1 mm, condition assessed with your wear and failure data. Before-and-after report included.

04

Reconditioning

Cut out and realign joints, fit a fitting rail or replace the rail, with or without support. The individual case decides.

05

Site supervision

Condition analysis, on-site support and acceptance for new builds and conversions, including site and welding coordination.

06

Training

Hands-on training in welding and grinding of rail systems, consulting for high-bay warehouses, production and crane systems.

07

Process engineering

Feasibility studies, custom joint connections, proprietary rail profiles and grinding rigs for narrow aisles.

FAQ

What customers ask before the first job.

Answered briefly. Everything else we settle in a call.

How fast can you be on site?
In urgent cases usually within a few days. We also work on weekends and during production breaks so your plant is down as briefly as possible.
Does the warehouse have to stop for the work?
No. We grind aisle by aisle. The closed aisle is usually free again after one shift while the rest of the warehouse keeps running.
How long does grinding one aisle take?
Depending on length, condition and access we plan one shift per aisle and rail. After the survey we give you a fixed schedule.
How do I know the rail needs grinding?
Typical signs are increasing running noise, vibration on the stacker crane, visible corrugation on the running surface and rising wear on the wheels.
Do I get documentation?
Yes. You receive a before-and-after report with measurements to 0.1 mm and a recommendation for the next maintenance interval.
Do you also weld rail joints?
Yes. Settled or broken joints are welded properly, depending on the profile with MIG/MAG, CMT, TIG or manual arc, and then ground flush.

Grinding is precision work.

Every tenth of a millimetre decides smoothness, wear and the rail's service life.
